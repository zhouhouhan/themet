# 00 · 世界总设定（Master World Prompt）

**用途**：描述整个 vgallery 世界的一张"定调图/主视觉"prompt（happyoyster 式可交互世界
的方向）。生成整体概念图或对外介绍用；具体空间按 01/02 逐个生成。

## Prompt

```text
Master concept art — the key establishing shot of a walkable museum world.

A vast Beaux-Arts art museum at golden hour, rendered as one continuous
explorable world: the monumental Great Hall with its cream limestone walls
and Grand Staircase flows into a long European Paintings gallery with deep
burgundy walls and gilded frames; at the heart of the gallery stands a tall
freestanding glass vitrine lit from within, holding a small baroque gold
crown set with clusters of emeralds; some paintings on the walls are subtly
ALIVE — clouds drifting, water rippling inside the frames; one large
painting glows like an open doorway into a sunlit Venetian square, revealing
the world inside the canvas.

A 16-year-old East Asian male student guide — short black hair, dark charcoal
hoodie, navy backpack, holding a slim pocket audio-guide device — stands
small in the middle distance, seen from behind, about to lead the way.

Camera: wide cinematic establishing shot from a slightly elevated
third-person view, one-point perspective down the central axis of hall and
gallery.

Style: 16:9 widescreen, photorealistic architectural visualization with
painterly touches inside the living paintings, warm elegant color grading,
quiet awe-inspiring atmosphere. IMPORTANT: no readable text anywhere.
```
