# 04 · Wheat Field with Cypresses 画中世界（旗舰沉浸）

**用途**：走进梵高《麦田里的丝柏》的沉浸世界（图生 3D 旗舰）。
**工作流（参考图版）**：在 ChatGPT **同一条消息**里上传原画 + 贴下面 prompt。

**原画（CC0，本地已存）**：
- 本地：`assets/paintings/wheat-field-with-cypresses.jpg`（4000×3184）
- 源：https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/ep/original/DP-42549-001.jpg
- 藏品页：metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/436535（Gallery 822）

## 故事版 prompt（+ 上传原画一起发）

```text
Attached is Vincent van Gogh's oil painting "Wheat Field with Cypresses"
(1889). Use it as the STRICT visual reference for palette, elements and
brushwork.

Turn this exact painting into a walkable world: a concept art of the painted
scene expanded into an immersive 3D landscape a visitor can walk through,
faithful to the painting — the brilliant chrome-yellow rolling wheat field,
the two enormous flame-like dark green cypresses, the swirling deep-blue and
white sky, the soft Provençal hills and distant farmhouse, bright
Mediterranean light.

Subject in foreground: a 16-year-old East Asian male student, seen ONLY from
behind — short black hair, dark charcoal hoodie, small navy backpack, holding
a slim pocket audio-guide device in one hand, standing at the start of a
narrow dirt path that cuts through the wheat ahead of him, leading toward the
cypresses — this path is the walking route through the world.

Camera: cinematic over-the-shoulder third-person shot — camera just behind
and slightly above the student's shoulder; his back occupies the lower-center
third of the frame, the wheat field and cypresses opening up ahead along a
strong one-point perspective.

Style: 16:9 widescreen. The whole world keeps visible oil-painting
brushstroke texture on every surface — sky, wheat, hills — exactly matching
the attached painting's brushwork, like walking inside the actual canvas.
NOT photorealistic. No readable text anywhere.
```

## 干净版（故事版满意后，同对话接着贴）

```text
Now generate the exact same scene from the same camera position, but with the
student removed and the dirt path empty — a clean people-free plate of the
painted world.
```

## 挑图标准

- 世界配色/元素与原画一一对应（黄麦田、火焰丝柏、漩涡蓝天、远处山丘农舍）
- 全画面保持梵高笔触，不能滑向照片感或 3D 渲染感
- 土路清晰笔直穿过麦田（3D 行走路线）
- 学生背影规范、零文字
