# 05 · The Harvesters 画中世界（第二沉浸）

**用途**：走进勃鲁盖尔《收割者》的沉浸世界。
**工作流（参考图版）**：在 ChatGPT **同一条消息**里上传原画 + 贴下面 prompt。

**原画（CC0，本地已存）**：
- 本地：`assets/paintings/the-harvesters.jpg`（3811×2809）
- 源：https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/ep/original/DP119115.jpg
- 藏品页：metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/435809（Gallery 638）

## 故事版 prompt（+ 上传原画一起发）

```text
Attached is Pieter Bruegel the Elder's oil painting "The Harvesters" (1565).
Use it as the STRICT visual reference for palette, composition elements and
brushwork.

Turn this exact painting into a walkable world: a concept art of the painted
scene expanded into an immersive 3D landscape a visitor can walk through,
faithful to the painting — the vast ripened golden wheat field on rolling
hills under a hazy summer sky, the tall slender pear tree in the near
foreground with peasants resting and eating in its shade, the winding path
leading down toward the distant village with its church spire, the far bay
with tiny sailing ships dissolving in summer haze, harvesters with scythes
working mid-distance.

Subject in foreground: a 16-year-old East Asian male student, seen ONLY from
behind — short black hair, dark charcoal hoodie, small navy backpack, holding
a slim pocket audio-guide device in one hand, standing on the winding field
path at the top of the slope — this path is the walking route down into the
scene.

Camera: cinematic over-the-shoulder third-person shot — camera just behind
and slightly above the student's shoulder; his back occupies the lower-center
third of the frame, the wheat landscape and valley opening up ahead in
one-point perspective.

Style: 16:9 widescreen. The whole world keeps the look of a 16th-century
Netherlandish oil painting — muted greens, ochres, soft summer haze, fine
visible brushwork on every surface — exactly matching the attached painting,
like walking inside Bruegel's actual canvas. NOT photorealistic. No readable
text anywhere.
```

## 干净版（故事版满意后，同对话接着贴）

```text
Now generate the exact same scene from the same camera position, but with the
student removed and the path empty — the distant peasants may stay, a clean
foreground plate of the painted world.
```

## 挑图标准

- 世界配色/元素与原画对应：金麦田、右侧梨树+树荫农人、蜿蜒土路、远处村庄教堂尖、右侧远海帆船
- 16 世纪油画质感（土黄/橄榄绿灰调、夏日薄雾），与 04 梵高浓彩拉开差异
- 土路可读、通向村庄（行走路线）
- 零文字
